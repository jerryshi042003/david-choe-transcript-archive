01 / THE BET

Thirty days to become real.

Respect will not come from explaining the whole dream. It comes from making one complete thing that puts taste, tennis knowledge, and execution on the line.

  1. Days 1–7 Tom makes 100 sketches. Jerry marks only what is usable on a tennis court. No committee design.
  2. Days 8–14 Make three physical prototypes. Choose one by wear, movement, and whether a player would keep it.
  3. Days 15–21 Put the best object on one real player at one real court. Shoot a 60–90 second film with no brand mythology voice-over.
  4. Days 22–30 Give ten pieces to people Jerry genuinely respects. Ask for nothing. Record only what happens naturally.

Progress is saved only on this device. The schedule is a proposed execution test, not a commitment made on Tom’s behalf.

02

Sean Parker wanted the work before Facebook.

Choe’s version reframes the famous stock choice: Parker had pursued his art during the Napster years, so the later offer was not a random lottery ticket.

Apply: build work people remember before you need the relationship.

Ranch I · 34:10 ↗

03

The ice-cream-shop buyer and the lost paintings.

A Marin couple finds his low-priced work through an ice-cream shop, offers more than $20K for two large paintings, and the paintings disappear from his truck in a storm on the drive north.

Apply: humble placement can create the break; fulfillment still has to survive reality.

Ranch II · 54:00 ↗

04

Buy the young artist before consensus.

Choe recalls buying one of the few works sold at an early Banksy LA show even though stencil art was not his taste; the lesson is support, not investment hindsight.

Apply: pay, wear, show up, and circulate work while it still matters to the maker.

Ranch II · 1:13:17 ↗

05

Give the best work, not the leftover.

Choe argues that a young artist should give heroes the best painting, work free for a bounded period, and let the work create demand before charging.

Apply: ten gifts, no transactional ask, no “shirt for post” bargain.

Ranch III · 44:32 ↗
