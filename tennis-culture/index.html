DAVID CHOE — GAMBLING / RESTRAINT

  • Power Ranger / Las Vegas / desert. Choe says he dated an actress who had played a Power Ranger when he was 21. After a fight on the drive back from Las Vegas, she left him in the desert without money. He describes the heat, ripping his shirt, and later hitchhiking to Los Angeles with a couple. During the ordeal, he says he recognized that greed and the inability to stop—not only losing bets—were the central problem. I started figuring out a way of how to never lose in Vegas. DVDASA 038 · 00:41:49–01:01:14.
  • The system. He did not claim a betting edge. He brought Harry Kim, paid him, and made Harry physically remove winnings and drag him away. Years later he reframed the question as: How can I feel good … and then stop? Rich Roll · 01:04:14–01:06:36.
  • Walk away. He applies the same rule to gambling, art, work, and relationships: do not remain only because the good part might return. Learn to walk away. Ranch II · 01:28:28–01:31:39.

DAVID CHOE — FACEBOOK / MONEY / RIGHTS

  • Not a random stock miracle. Sean Parker had emailed Choe for years after Napster because he wanted the work but could not then afford it. When Facebook had funding, Parker returned. Choe called accepting stock instead of cash a huge gamble. Ranch I · 00:34:09–00:37:59.
