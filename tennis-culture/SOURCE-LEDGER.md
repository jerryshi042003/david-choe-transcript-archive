## Rights decision

The downloadable OPEN COURT review reel uses only newly authored typography, summaries, timestamps, and citations. It contains no copied podcast, YouTube, Vimeo, film, or television audio/video.

A footage-based compilation is **rights-blocked** until the relevant source owner is identified and a lawful reuse basis is confirmed. Commercial sources remain links or timestamp indexes; *Dirty Hands* dialogue remains withheld under the archive policy.

## Honest completeness boundary

“Every David Choe video on the internet” is a moving, non-finite target. Each discovery must be entered as included, duplicate, unavailable, excluded, or rights-blocked. The corpus numbers above describe this release snapshot, not universal completeness.
