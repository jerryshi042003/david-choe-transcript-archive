# Mike Cherman interviews — coverage ledger

Updated 10 August 2026. This is part of the [Jerry / Tom working record](../) and the broader Mike/Shingo source work.

## Inclusion rule

Included when the public YouTube video makes Mike Cherman a featured speaker and preserves a substantive first-person answer, conversation, panel contribution, tour, or workshop. The main list holds direct interviews, podcasts, and panels. A separate adjacent list holds first-person material that is useful but should not be mislabeled as an interview.

Excluded: brief news mentions; ads and event recaps without substantive first-person speech; commentary profiles assembled by someone else; duplicate excerpts when the full episode is present; videos where Mike interviews another guest; unrelated people with similar names; and videos unavailable to the public.

YouTube searches were run on 10 August 2026, up to the first 50–75 results per query:

- `Mike Cherman interview`

The exact name, both given-name variants, and his three major public brand handles were used because no single query returned the complete set. Results were deduplicated by YouTube video ID and manually classified. The manifest is the machine-readable retained denominator.

“Every” is bounded to this reproducible sweep. YouTube search is ranked, personalized, mutable, and not a complete global index. A future upload, deleted/private item, badly titled video, or result ranked below the inspected window can remain undiscovered.

## Transcript and editorial method

- 25 videos use the public English caption track returned by YouTube.
- `jWC4joiHaTY` is publicly listed on YouTube but age-gated to unauthenticated tools. Its transcript was generated locally from the publisher's public Acast audio file, without using Jerry's browser account.
- `-_xq_Hk3Q6Q` and `R0RQYZdFEYM` have no usable public English caption track. Their transcripts were generated locally with whisper.cpp `small.en`.
- Full access transcripts remain only in ignored `.private/mike-cherman-transcripts/` storage. They were used to build the tracked interview pages but are not republished.
- Every tracked interview page contains an original synopsis, source-faithful paraphrased notes, direct timestamp links, and a separate practical implication for Tom. The notes remove caption repetition and speech noise; they do not present invented speaker labels or verbatim full captions.
- The editorial notes are reviewed research aids, not substitutes for the recording. Verify the linked source before quoting Mike.

The underlying access transcripts are not publication-ready quotations. Automatic captions and ASR mishear proper names, compress crosstalk, and can omit punctuation. Verify the linked source before quoting.
