Cleaned transcript from Jerry’s account

Two weeks ago I called Tom. He was in Bellevue, had time, and kept saying there was nothing to do. I told him that the most boring places can be where you build something yourself. I told him to talk to Mike Cherman and Shingo Arai because they are already doing the tennis and clothing work we respect.

We were on FaceTime while I was playing. I did not have a tripod, so I balanced my phone on the ball mower on the other side of the court. I served and Tom returned through the phone.

Two weeks later, we have ideas but nothing to send Mike or Shingo. That is the problem. I also do not know whether I can take time away from my normal job. Maybe the realistic version is that I stay in LA and work with Mike.

I do not have a strong desire to work in clothing for its own sake. What I want is to travel, play tennis in different countries, meet people, and learn how they live. Tennis is how I enter a place. Clothes may give the project a reason to exist, but they are not the final reason.
